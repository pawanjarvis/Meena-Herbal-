Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before ordering.

Do you offer Cash on Delivery?

Yes, Cash on Delivery is available across most PIN codes in India. You can select COD at checkout. Online payment is also supported via Razorpay (UPI, cards, netbanking).

How long does delivery take?

Metro cities: 3–5 business days. Other cities: 5–7 business days. Remote PIN codes may take up to 10 business days. You will receive a tracking link once dispatched.

What is your return policy?

You can request a return within 7 days of delivery for damaged, defective, or incorrect items. Products must be unused and in original packaging. Opened herbal items cannot be returned for hygiene reasons.

Are your products safe for sensitive skin?

Our products are made with gentle, plant-based ingredients. However, we recommend doing a small patch test first, especially if you have sensitive skin or allergies. Consult a dermatologist if unsure.

Do you ship outside India?

Currently we ship only within India. For special requests, please contact us on WhatsApp.
